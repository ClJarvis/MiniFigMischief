Please enjoy my photography. Minifig Mischief is the new name for Yoda Archives Toy Photography. I wanted a more distinct name for what I do. With the new name, people will instantly know it's about Mini figures. Yoda archives will now be more fandom blogging while Minifig Mischief will be more photography focused. I will start by sharing some of my favorite images from the old site.
I am also on PixelFed, an ad free fedivesre photosharing site.

WereWolf Web.

Above is the Canadian Werewolf. Lego Wolf Guy from LEGO Minifigure Halloween Collection. This was series 14 of their Collectible mini-figures series. Note: CMF series 5 had a lumberjack fig that wore a similar plaid shirt and jeans. Canonically these are the same character. I shot this image last fall as part of a series of werewolf toy pics.

Retro Space Hero and Heroine.

The Retro Space Hero is on of my all time favorite mini figs. He’d been missing for a while, I stored him in a box of bricks that I was collecting to make him a color themed space ship. I finally found him and had to pose him with his counterpart from newer CMF 26. They look cool together.
